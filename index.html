Our Story

Beauty rooted in Ugandan tradition

Abral Makeup, Veiling and Bridals was born from a deep love for Ugandan celebrations, the colour of kwanjula, the grace of a bridal procession, the joy that fills every ceremony. We bring professional artistry and heartfelt dedication to every client we serve, whether it's your first makeup lesson or your wedding day. Every look we create is an ode to elegance.
